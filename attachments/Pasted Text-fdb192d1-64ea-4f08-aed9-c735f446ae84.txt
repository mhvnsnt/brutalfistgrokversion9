Here is how AAA games solve this, and the exact directive to force Rocket to revert the damage.
The AAA Architecture (Master Rigging)
Games like WWE, Tekken, and Urban Reign never let the game engine guess how a model is put together. They use a Master Rig Architecture:
 * Immutable Bind Pose: The engine is strictly forbidden from altering the skeleton's structure. The bone hierarchy is treated as an absolute mathematical constant.
 * Dumb Meshes: The 3D skin (the mesh) does not think. It is just glued to the bones.
 * The Mixer dictates, the Bones follow: The animation state machine only talks to the Master Skeleton. When the skeleton throws a punch, the skin naturally deforms.
